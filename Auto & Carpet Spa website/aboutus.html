Welcome to Auto & Carpet Spa by Jim

Reviving your ride and home with professional care, passion, and precision in Kenya.

Our Story

Founded by Jim, Auto & Carpet Spa by Jim began with a simple mission: to deliver top-tier car washing and deep carpet/upholstery cleaning services that exceed expectations.

With years of experience in detailing and a passion for perfection, we combine eco-friendly products, advanced equipment, and meticulous attention to detail. Whether it's restoring the shine to your vehicle after Nairobi's dusty roads or refreshing your home/office carpets, we treat every job like it's our own.

Today, we proudly serve families, professionals, and businesses across Nairobi and beyond — turning "dirty" into "like new" every single day.

What We Offer

Exterior & Interior Car Wash

High-pressure foam wash, hand drying, wheel & tire shine, interior vacuuming & wipe-down.

Full Car Detailing

Paint correction, clay bar, wax/sealant, engine bay cleaning — showroom-ready results.

Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning

Deep extraction cleaning for car seats, floor mats, home/office carpets — stain removal & odor elimination.
